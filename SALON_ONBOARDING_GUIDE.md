# CLOUD SALON – Salon Owner Onboarding Guide
## Cloud Technologies | Quick-Start Manual for Salon Owners & Managers

Welcome to **Cloud Salon**, your all-in-one salon management and billing software. This guide walks you through setting up your salon in under 10 minutes.

---

### Step 1: Login to Your Salon Account
1. Open your web browser on your PC, laptop, or tablet and visit:
   `https://salon.cloudtechnologiess.in`
2. Enter your registered **Owner Email** and **Password** provided by Cloud Technologies.
3. Tap **Sign In**. You will enter your salon's private workspace.

---

### Step 2: Complete Salon Profile & Branding
1. Navigate to **Settings** from the sidebar.
2. In **Salon Profile**:
   - Verify your **Salon Name** and **Legal Name**.
   - Confirm your **Address**, **City**, and **Pincode**.
   - Set your **Operating Hours** (e.g. `10:00 AM` to `08:00 PM`).
   - Configure your **Thermal Header & Footer** (e.g. *"Thank you for visiting! For appointments WhatsApp +91 9822XXXXXX"*).
3. Tap **Save Profile Settings**.

---

### Step 3: Add Your Salon Treatments & Prices
1. Navigate to **Services** in the sidebar.
2. Use the default categories (*Hair Styling, Skin Care, Spa & Massage, Makeup*) or add custom categories.
3. Click **"+ Add Service"**:
   - Service Name (e.g. *Hydra Facial, Keratin Hair Spa, Layered Haircut*).
   - Price in ₹.
   - Duration in minutes (e.g. `30` min, `60` min).
4. Save the service. Your services immediately appear in POS Billing and the Appointment Calendar!

---

### Step 4: Add Stylists & Team Accounts
1. Navigate to **Staff & Comm.** from the sidebar.
2. Click **"+ Add Staff"**:
   - Staff Name & Contact Mobile.
   - Designation (e.g. *Senior Hair Stylist, Esthetician, Makeup Artist*).
   - Staff Color Tag (used for color-coded calendar appointments).
3. In **User Roles (RBAC)**:
   - Create login accounts for your receptionists or cashiers.
   - *Note on Professional Plan*: You can create up to 3 staff accounts (your Owner account does not count against this limit).

---

### Step 5: Fast B2C POS Billing (<15 Seconds)
When a walk-in client visits:
1. Tap **POS Billing** in the sidebar or hit the gold **Quick POS** button in the header.
2. Client name defaults to `Walk-in Client` (phone number optional).
3. Click the treatments or retail cosmetic products the client received.
4. Assign the stylist who performed the service.
5. Select payment mode: **UPI**, **CASH**, **CARD**, or **SPLIT**.
6. Tap **Complete & Print Receipt**!
   - Instant 80mm thermal receipt or A4 PDF is generated.
   - Stock is automatically decremented for any retail products sold.

---

### Step 6: Install as Progressive Web App (PWA)
1. On **Chrome** or **Edge** (Windows/Mac):
   - Look for the install icon in the address bar (or menu `Install Cloud Salon`).
2. On **Android** (Chrome):
   - Tap `Add to Home screen` or `Install App`.
3. On **iOS iPad / iPhone** (Safari):
   - Tap the Share button and select `Add to Home Screen`.
4. Cloud Salon will now run as a fast, full-screen native desktop or tablet application!
